How to play

The goal

Catch every golden fish in the level. When the last one is caught, the wormhole opens. Fly into it to finish the level. There are eight levels, and each one adds something new.

Leaping

Mochi walks around each planet as it spins. When you leap, Mochi flies straight out from the planet's centre. The dotted line shows the way. Planets pull Mochi in when Mochi passes close by.

Nine lives

You lose a life if Mochi drifts past the dotted edge of space or touches a danger below. Mochi comes back on the last planet Mochi stood on, and fish already caught stay caught.

What you will meet

  • Golden fishCatch them all to open the wormhole. 100 points each.
  • Cosmic yarnA bonus you don't need to finish. 300 points.
  • WormholeOpens when every fish is caught. Fly in to finish.
  • Moon rockCircles planets or drifts by. Costs a life.
  • Space dogPatrols in a saucer. Costs a life.
  • SunPulls Mochi in. Landing on it costs a life.
  • Black holeBends Mochi's path. Its middle costs a life.

Stars and score

Each level gives up to three stars: one for finishing, one for losing no lives, one for beating the par time. Fish, yarn, finishing, time left under par and a clean run all add to your score.
