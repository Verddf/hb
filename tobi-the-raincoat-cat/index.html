How to play

The storm scattered the sun. Run and jump through four stages and ring the bell at the end of each one. Reach the bell higher up its post for a bigger bonus. If you fall, you start again from the last lamp you passed.

← →
Move. A and D work too.
Space
Jump. Hold it longer to jump higher. Z or ↑ also jump.
Shift
Run while you hold it, for longer jumps. X also runs. Every stage can be finished without running.
P
Pause. M turns the music on or off.

With the umbrella, hold jump in the air to glide. On a phone the buttons appear under the screen: slide your thumb onto both to run and jump together. A gamepad works as well.

Four stages

  1. Drizzle Meadow: stumps, ponds and your first snail.
  2. Snail Hollow: lifts over a deep pit and a floor of crystal spikes.
  3. Nimbus Heights: springs, falling rafts and a rainbow bridge.
  4. Thunder Keep: lava, turning ember chains and stone crushers.

Field guide

  • GrumbleA sulky raindrop. Land on it.
  • SnailLand on it, then kick the shell. Mind the rebound.
  • PrickleSpikes on top. Jump over it, or send a shell.
  • FlitDrifts in the air. Bounce off it to go higher.
  • HopperLeaps at you. Stomp it between hops.
  • Sun blockHit it from below for coins and items.
  • UmbrellaGlide, smash crates, and take one hit safely.
  • Sun dropFor ten seconds nothing can hurt you.
  • Sun shardThree are hidden in every stage.
  • CoinOne hundred of them earn an extra try.

You get three tries per game. Stages you have reached, your best score and your sound settings are remembered in this browser only.
